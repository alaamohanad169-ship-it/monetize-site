Best Tech Practices to Avoid AI Catastrophes 
Implementing Strict Access Controls
The first line of defense in preventing unauthorized access is implementing strict access controls. This ensures that AI agents are granted only the permissions necessary for their designated tasks.
#### Example of Access Control Practices
- Limit Permissions: Grant AI access only to the databases it requires for its specific functions.
- Role-Based Access: Establish clear roles within your organization that dictate what data and commands can be executed by the AI.
Regular Audits and Monitoring
Conducting routine audits of AI activities can help identify abnormalities before they escalate into major issues. This includes monitoring the commands executed by AI agents to ensure they align with expected behaviors.
#### Tools for Monitoring AI
1. Logging Tools: Utilize logging systems to track all actions performed by AI agents.
2. AI Monitoring Software: Implement dedicated software solutions that provide insights into AI decision-making processes and outcomes.
Training and Awareness
Team members involved in the development process should receive training on best practices for working with AI. This education should encompass an understanding of both the limitations and capabilities of the AI tools being utilized.
#### Training Programs
